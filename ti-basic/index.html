On the home screen you can also type math like 2+3 or 5→A and press ENTER. PRGM types prgmHOME for you, and ALPHA types the green letter above the next key.

Keyboard: arrows, Enter, Esc = CLEAR, Backspace = DEL, digits and letters type, Ctrl+B = ON (break).
