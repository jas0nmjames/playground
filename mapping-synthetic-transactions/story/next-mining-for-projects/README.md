## What's planned

- **Rank the frictions.** Every run's `summary.md` already ranks the ten costliest frictions by hours, fees and lost wages and by who they hit hardest; on the first day, fees, distance and waiting led. Rank them across v2's typed-day year and the March month, where chains across days (an unpaid bill to a shutoff, an overdraft to a late rent) change the cost.
- **Recheck the project leads.** The [evidence report](../../evidence/report.md#4-project-leads-backed-by-both-the-simulation-and-the-evidence) named nine leads backed by both the first day and real evidence, from a refund-day fee check to payday-timed low-balance alerts and a ghost-bus log. Each needs checking against v2, which added most of the jobs the first day was missing.
- **The field visits.** Eight visits, one or two per district, each timed to a busy moment: a Driver Services center, Five Points MARTA, a highway-exit gas station, a laundromat and check-casher strip, a rural Dollar General and pharmacy, a school pickup line, an ER waiting room and a farm stand. The [city design](../00-the-idea/synthetic-mid-america-city-design-v0.1.md#field-visit-plan) has the plan and the [evidence report](../../evidence/report.md#5-field-visit-prompts) what to watch for at each. Notes go in the same record schema, tagged as observed.

Alongside, from the documents of earlier milestones: households drawn from Census microdata (v3), circumstances that drive the simulator so the lens's "what if" becomes a second run, and loops tested against chains across many days ([systems lens open questions](../04-the-refined-city/medianville-systems-lens.md#open-questions)).
