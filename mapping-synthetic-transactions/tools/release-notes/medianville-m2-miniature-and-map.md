Three visual directions were prototyped in the Claude app (Tiles, Tabletop and Six Streets), Tabletop was chosen, and it became v3, Miniature and Map: two views of one city, a tabletop 3D town and the same town as a printed topographic sheet, with one record language across both. `real-day.js` folds the run's six districts onto v3's own town. No viewer v2 shipped; the prototypes were the second round.

- **Decided:** two views of one city; one record language (a colour, a line and a head for each kind of record); failures never sampled; a finance-app interface.
