The archive

Earlier versions of the viewer, kept as they shipped. They still play the first run of the day, when the city was called Halvern.

New work lands in the latest viewer. How each version was made, and what came between them, is in the story. Each version changed only in its relative paths and an “Archived version” link back to the latest.

  • The night map

    M1 One day · shipped November 17, 2026 · first viewer (v1)

    Every record of Wednesday, March 3, 2027 drawn as a light crossing an abstract map of the city’s six districts, with a timeline, a live ticker and a sheet for each record. It read 201,753 records straight from the zip in a few seconds, and stayed at the front of the project until the latest viewer took its place on November 20.

  • Miniature and Map, as first shipped

    M2 Miniature and Map · shipped November 17, 2026 · v3

    Two views of one small city: a tabletop 3D town and the same town as a printed topographic sheet, with one record language across both. It was chosen from three visual directions prototyped in the Claude app, and it is the viewer the latest one grew out of.
