# Districts and circumstances

**Composite.** Every block, circumstance and loop here is a composite of documented US patterns on an invented block, not a real place. The district tier's figures are national or from documented cases and say so, and nothing local is named. The file's own note: "Composite circumstances on invented blocks, drawn from documented US patterns. Loops are hypotheses. The simulator reads 'select' and each pattern's 'match' to tag records; the lens reads everything else."

Rendered from https://medianville.netlify.app/systems-lens/blocks.json (version 1), the file the systems lens (https://medianville.netlify.app/systems-lens/) draws and the simulator tags records from. Layers run from the fastest-changing to the slowest: Economic, Political, Built & natural, Social & cultural, Historical. Evidence ids (E…) are findings in the evidence folder on GitHub (https://github.com/jas0nmjames/playground/blob/main/mapping-synthetic-transactions/evidence/evidence.csv), third-party material that is not under this project's license. No what-if scenarios are modeled: a loop is a hypothesis, not a model result.
